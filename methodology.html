How AROM TRADE signals work

Every AROM TRADE signal comes from a fixed set of rules applied to real market candles. The engine either finds a setup that passes every rule, or it stays quiet.

Three trading styles

  • Scalping works on the 5-minute and 1-minute charts. Trades last minutes to a few hours.
  • Intraday and Swing work on higher timeframes and hold trades longer, from hours to days.

The checklist

A signal is graded A+ only when every rule in its checklist passes, and the AI Desk shows the checklist each signal passed. The scalping checklist, for example:

  • 5-minute trend: EMA 20 and EMA 50 agree, and the slope confirms it.
  • The 1-minute EMA 20 and EMA 50 agree with the 5-minute trend.
  • Price pulls back into a key level: a support or resistance zone, a trendline, or a breakout being retested.
  • A candle closes through the trigger at that level.
  • No high-impact USD news within 30 minutes.
  • The stop sits beyond the zone, between 0.3 and 1.5 times the 5-minute ATR.
  • There is at least 2R of room to the next zone.

News lock

Signals pause 30 minutes before and 15 minutes after high-impact USD news, when prices can jump both ways.

Stops and targets

Each signal gives an entry, a stop loss and its targets. R is the distance from entry to stop, and the targets depend on the style. After TP1 the stop moves to the entry price.

How results are counted

  • Stop hit before TP1: −1R.
  • TP1 reached: half the position closes at TP1 and the stop moves to entry, so the trade scores at least half of TP1: +0.5R scalping, +0.75R intraday, +1R swing.
  • TP2 reached: the second half closes at TP2: +1.5R scalping, +2.25R intraday, +3R swing in total.

Results are counted before spread and fees, and your broker's prices can differ slightly from ours. The live numbers are on the track record page.

Shadow mode

A new strategy starts in shadow mode. Its signals are recorded and scored but not sent to members. It is published only after about 30 signals show that it holds up.

What rules cannot do

Rules remove guesswork; they do not remove risk. Signals lose often, and past results do not predict future ones. Please read the risk disclosure.
