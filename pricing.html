A+ confirmation checklist
    How the engine decides
    1. Trend: on M15 the close is above EMA50, EMA50 is above EMA200 and rising → only BUY (mirror for SELL). Ranging = no trade.
    2. Pullback: on M5, price dips back into the EMA20–EMA50 zone within the last 6 candles without breaking deep through EMA50.
    3. Trigger: the signal candle closes back with the trend as a bullish/bearish engulfing, a pin bar, or a strong momentum candle.
    4. Filters: RSI 14 in the momentum zone and turning, higher-low structure, normal ATR volatility, room to the next swing, London/New York session, no high-impact USD news within 30 minutes.
    5. Risk: stop 0.25 ATR beyond the pullback extreme (must be 0.8–3 ATR). TP1 = 1R, TP2 = 2R, TP3 = 3R. Move stop to entry after TP1.
    6. Only when all 11 checks pass is the setup graded A+ and a signal released. An "A" grade is shown for watching only.
