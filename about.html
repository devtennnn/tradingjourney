About AROM TRADE

AROM TRADE is a trading journal and signal desk built for traders in Cambodia. It works in English and Khmer, shows every time in Cambodia time (UTC+7) and takes payment with KHQR.

What we make

  • Trading journal: log trades with screenshots and see your win rate, profit factor, R multiples, best sessions and equity curve. Free.
  • AI Desk: rule-based setups for gold (XAUUSD), Bitcoin and other markets, each with an entry, a stop loss and targets, plus a buy or sell bias from USD news.
  • News and alerts: high-impact US economic releases, with alerts in the app, on your phone and in Telegram.
  • Broker sync: your MT5 or MT4 balance, open positions and closed trades copied into the journal, read-only.
  • AROM Intelligence: market briefings and an AI analyst that explains verified market data (Ultra plan).

How we work

  • Signals come from fixed rules applied to real market candles, not from opinions. See how signals work.
  • A new strategy runs in shadow mode first: its signals are recorded and scored but not published until about 30 of them show how it behaves.
  • Every published signal is counted in the public track record, losses included.
  • Nothing on AROM TRADE is financial advice. Please read the risk disclosure.

Your data and your money

  • Your journal belongs to your account and is kept private in our cloud database.
  • Broker sync only reads your account. The AROM Sync EA cannot place, change or close a trade, and the phone option uses your read-only investor password, which we never store.
  • Plans are paid 30 days at a time with KHQR and never renew on their own.
